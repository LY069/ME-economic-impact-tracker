Scenario explorer

Move the drivers and the model recomputes. The four channels are an income transfer abroad on net energy imports, a price-induced (voluntary) demand response, a physical shortfall that must be rationed once substitution and reserve draw are exhausted, and the reserve runway. Price support is modelled as a switch because it is the real policy choice: it lowers CPI now and stops demand adjusting, which drains reserves faster.

Sensitivity: price against physical availability

The interaction is the story. A high price with open transit is an income shock that monetary and fiscal policy can cushion; a low price with closed transit is a rationing shock that they cannot. Cells show the GDP impact in percentage points.
